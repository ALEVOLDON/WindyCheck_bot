🌬️ User Guide & Manual
Telegram Bot «WindyCheck Bot»

Personal assistant for wind tracking, forecasts, analytics charts, and real-time alerts

1. Key Features

  • 🌬️ Current Wind: Real-time speed (m/s), direction (degrees + compass indicator 🧭), gusts, and air temperature.
  • 📅 24-Hour Forecast: Hourly wind condition breakdown for the next 24 hours with charts.
  • 📊 Graphic Analytics: Automated generation of dual-panel PNG infographics showing wind dynamics and Wind Rose diagrams.
  • 🗺️ Interactive Maps: Direct link generation to live weather and wind maps on Windy.com for target locations.
  • 📍 Saved Spots (Favorites): Store your favorite spots in SQLite database for 1-click access.
  • 📊 Spot Comparison: Compare wind dynamics across all your saved spots on a single unified timeline.
  • 🔔 Automated Alerts: Background weather monitoring loop every 30 minutes with instant push notifications when wind speed exceeds threshold limits.

3. Step-by-Step Guide

📌 1. Quick Wind Check
Send the bot any city name (e.g., "Dahab"). You will receive a rich data card with live weather parameters and interactive buttons to refresh or view live maps.
📌 2. Setting Up Wind Alerts
Type /alert 10. The bot will check weather across your favorite spots every 30 minutes and send an instant push notification when wind speed hits 10 m/s or higher.
📌 3. Comparing Watersport Spots
Save your favorite locations using /track [city]. Tap the "Comparative Chart" button to render a unified timeline chart displaying wind trends for all tracked spots.
